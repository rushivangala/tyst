**Tyst: the keyboard that writes like you and never goes online.**

Android 17 keyboard for the Pixel 10 Pro (runs on Android 12 and later).

### Install
1. Download `tyst-v1.0.0.apk` below on your phone and open it. Allow installs from your browser or Files app when Android asks.
2. If Play Protect offers to scan it, tap **Scan app**, then **Install**. If it says the developer is unknown, tap **More details › Install anyway**.
3. Open **Tyst** and follow the setup to switch it on and choose it as your keyboard.

**Upgrading from a test build?** Uninstall the old Tyst first. Test builds were signed with a temporary key, and Android won't install over them. Later releases update in place.

### What's in it
- Predictions and autocorrect that learn how you write, with backspace to undo a correction.
- Swedish words, names and places recognised inside English and never "corrected", plus å ä ö by long-press or an optional row.
- Writing tools: Rewrite (My style, Warmer, Formal, Shorter, Longer) and Proofread, all on the phone.
- Private memory (opt-in) for addresses, booking references, phone numbers and emails.
- Password manager suggestions from Android autofill, copied codes with a countdown, and the last copied text as a paste chip.
- On-device voice typing, emoji, one-handed mode, light/dark, wallpaper or curated accents.
- No internet permission. Learned data is encrypted and left out of backups. Settings › Privacy proof shows the permissions read live from Android.
