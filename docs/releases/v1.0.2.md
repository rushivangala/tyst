**NordPass suggestions now stay in the keyboard.**

### Fixed
- Password manager pills (for example "Matchi.com · your email" from NordPass) no longer vanish a moment after appearing. Android follows the password manager's answer with an empty one for the same field, and Tyst was clearing the pills when it arrived. Tyst now keeps them until you leave the field.
- The Autofill check in Passwords & codes now writes one line per answer instead of one per pill, and notes which field opened.

### Install
Download `tyst-v1.0.2.apk` below and open it. It installs over v1.0.1 and keeps your settings.
