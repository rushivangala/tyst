Turn 1 · Name and launcher icon

Four names, one icon each

All short Swedish words that read cleanly in English. Icons are Android adaptive icons on the 108dp grid, shown with the circle mask, the themed (monochrome) variant and at small sizes. Recommendation: 1a.

1aRecommended
tyst"teest" · quiet

Keeps the working name. A keycap whose spacebar is a flat, silent line: the keyboard that says nothing to the network.

1b
hem"hem" · home

Your words stay at home, on the phone. A key held inside a closed ring. Risk: "hem" also means a sewn edge in English.

1c
egen"ay-gen" · own

Leads with "writes like you". A text cursor signing its own line. Pronunciation is less obvious to English speakers.

1d
inne"in-neh" · inside

On-device, said plainly. A key inside the phone's rounded frame. Reads close to "inner" and "in".

Before committing: run a Google Play and trademark search on the chosen name in the keyboard and productivity categories.
