## Word lists

`core/src/main/resources/tyst/en_words.txt` and `sv_words.txt` are generated by `tools/gen_lexicon.py` from **wordfreq** 3.1.1 by Robyn Speer (https://github.com/rspeer/wordfreq). The wordfreq data is licensed under **CC BY-SA 4.0** (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). The derived lists are shared under the same licence.

`en_next.txt` (likely next words) and `en_unlisted.txt` (rare words no spell-checker lists) are generated by `tools/gen_bigrams.py` and `tools/gen_unlisted.py` from the English dictionaries in **symspellpy** 6.10.0 (https://github.com/mammothb/symspellpy, **MIT License**, © 2025 mmb L, © 2021 Wolf Garbe). SymSpell built those dictionaries from **Google Books Ngram** data, which Google publishes under **CC BY 3.0** (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/), and from SCOWL word lists.

The other files in that folder (misspellings, proper nouns, Swedish words, phrases) were written for Tyst. The benchmark messages in `core/src/test/resources/bench/` were written for Tyst too.
